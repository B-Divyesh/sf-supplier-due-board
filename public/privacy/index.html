Privacy policy · 6 September 2026

How Due Board keeps data private

Due Board stores bills, paid notes, settings, and attachments in your browser’s IndexedDB storage on this device. It uses no account or tracking requests.

What leaves your device?

Nothing you enter is sent to us by the application. The web host may process standard connection logs such as an IP address when it serves application files. Due Board does not add tracking identifiers or analytics requests.

Your controls

Use Data controls on the board to export a complete JSON backup, export a spreadsheet-friendly CSV, import a backup, or delete every local record. Clearing browser site data can also remove the board, so keep an export if the records matter.

Attachments

Attachments remain inside local browser storage and are included in complete JSON backups. They are never uploaded by the application. Anyone with access to your device or exported backup may be able to view them.

For product or privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
