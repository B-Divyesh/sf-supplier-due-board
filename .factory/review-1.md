## Findings

### R1 — High — no one-click sample sandbox

The landing screen has no sample action. Fresh requests to both `/demo` and `/?demo=1` returned the normal empty board, with the home title and h1, no realistic sample bills, no persistent “Demo — sample data, nothing is saved” label, no **Reset demo**, and no **Start for real** control. There is also no `.factory/demo.md`.

This fails the demo-sandbox contract and prevents verification of the stated requirement that sample actions cannot touch real data. `/demo` is currently only the static navigation fallback, not a demo route.

### R2 — High — claims inventory is missing; 11 public claims have no declared runnable proof

`.factory/claims.json` is missing and the repository contains no `@claim:` test tags. Therefore there are no declared claim commands to run from a clean checkout, and the following public promises are untested under the required claims contract:

1. Records supplier, reference, amount, currency, and local-calendar due date.
2. Keeps a PDF or image attachment in browser storage.
3. Groups due/overdue/paid totals and supports search, filters, and sorting.
4. Records a paid date and payment note and can reopen a bill.
5. Prints the weekly supplier review.
6. Exports JSON and CSV, imports a backup, and deletes local data.
7. Rejects an invalid backup without changing current records.
8. Installs as a PWA and works offline after first use.
9. Has no account or remote application database.
10. Does not connect to a bank, move money, or verify payment.
11. Has no analytics/tracking requests.

The ordinary Playwright suite exercises much of this functionality, but that is not a substitute for the required one-test-per-public-claim inventory and commands. This review’s `untested_claim_count` is 11.

### R3 — Medium — first-screen copy does not meet the plain-words entry contract

The first screen omits the intended audience, does not offer the required three plain facts (privacy, offline, and price), and does not state the outcome of **Add a bill**. It also uses prohibited mood/metaphor copy: “Nothing more,” “Keep the paper. See the week,” and “Built with restraint.” The page calls the product a “Local supplier ledger,” then a “due board,” then uses “paper” as a conceptual label rather than consistently saying bills.

`.factory/copy-audit.md` is also missing, so the required sentence-length/banned-word audit and terminology table were not supplied.

### R4 — Medium — required public navigation structure is incomplete

There is no designed 404 route or response override. `https://supplier-due-board.sociobot.in/this-page-does-not-exist` returns HTTP 200 and renders the normal board, rather than a clear not-found page with a route-specific title and a way back. `sitemap.xml` returns HTTP 404. `robots.txt` exists but does not point crawlers to a sitemap.

These are missing required structure, not an expected deliberate HTTP 404.

### R5 — Low — required route metadata is incomplete

The landing HTML has a description and canonical link, but lacks Open Graph tags, Twitter card tags, and an Apple touch icon. The privacy and terms documents have route titles, but no description, canonical, Open Graph, or Twitter metadata. No real 1200×630 social image is present.
